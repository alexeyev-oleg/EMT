About the Company

Innovation and Precision in Every
Electronic Component

EMT — Electronics Manufacturing Technologies LTD — is a leading company in the field of manufacturing and soldering of electronic components, with over 20 years of industry experience.

We specialize in PCB assembly, manual and automated soldering (SMT), and complete integration of electronic products from the first stage to the final product. Our team consists of experienced engineers committed to maximum quality and precision.

Located in Park HiTech Yokneam, we provide fast, reliable and flexible service to companies from a wide range of industries — from medical devices to the defense industry.

What We Offer

Professional Services

SMT Assembly

Automated surface mount assembly with high precision, including BGA, QFN and fine-pitch components

Manual Soldering

Precise manual soldering for special components, repairs and prototypes by certified technicians

Assembly & Integration

Complete assembly of electronic products, including wiring, connections and integration into final enclosures

QA Testing

Comprehensive testing including ICT, AOI, X-Ray and functional tests to ensure maximum quality

Custom Solutions

Development and manufacture of custom electronic solutions, from prototype to serial production

Logistics & Supply
